## How to read this

- **[Launch](/internal-docs/product/roadmap/launch)** is a living checklist. Everything on it is
  in scope for the first release; the checkboxes make it easy to see how much is
  done and what still needs work. No dates or deadlines - just a picture of where
  we are and where we're going.
- **[Ideas](/internal-docs/product/roadmap/ideas)** is a backlog, not a plan. Nothing there is
  scheduled or designed. It's where possibilities get captured and explored;
  anything that graduates from an idea into committed work moves onto the Launch
  checklist (or a future release scope).
