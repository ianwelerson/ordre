## Service Types

### Overview

Each template defines a **service type** that determines the board's structure, expected lifecycle, and available features. The service type is a property of the template - when a member picks a template, the type comes with it. A single industry template (e.g. "Bike Shop") can offer more than one variant.

Quick and Full are both **linear**: a job starts, moves forward, and finishes. Recurring is **open-ended**, a standing relationship measured in visits rather than a job with an end. That difference drives most of what follows.

---

### Quick

For jobs completed in a single visit or within the same day. Minimal status tracking.

**Characteristics:**

- Simplified timeline - few updates expected (received → in progress → done)
- No phase tracker - the job doesn't pass through enough stages to justify one
- Lightweight board UI - optimised for fast creation and fast completion
- Board auto-archives after completion (configurable grace period)

**Examples:** bike wash, key cutting, phone screen protector, shoe shine, watch battery replacement, basic oil change.

---

### Full

The default type. For jobs that span multiple days and involve meaningful progress communication.

**Characteristics:**

- Full timeline with text, photo, and approval updates
- Phase tracker with template-defined stages (e.g. assessment → repair → quality check → ready)
- All board features available: chat, approvals, photo documentation
- Board remains active until explicitly completed or archived by a workspace member

**Examples:** watch repair, home renovation, bike overhaul, appliance repair, freelance design project.

---

### Recurring

For ongoing service relationships with repeating visits on a schedule. The board tracks a contract rather than a job, and stays open for its whole term.

**Characteristics:**

- **A cadence instead of a lifecycle** - weekly, biweekly, monthly, condition-triggered (e.g. "snowfall ≥ 5 cm"), or a free-text custom pattern
- **A visit list, not a phase tracker** - each visit is scheduled, then completed, rescheduled, missed, or skipped, and carries its own report
- **Per-visit reports** - a visit's timeline entry covers what was done on that visit, so the board reads as a service history
- **Reschedule requests in both directions** - a member can propose a new slot to the client, and a verified client can request one from the member
- **A contract term** - a start date and an optional end date, with an optional season label (e.g. "2025/26") for seasonal work
- **Pausable** - a contract can be suspended without archiving the board or losing its history

**Examples:** snow removal, lawn care, pool maintenance, recurring cleaning, grounds maintenance.

<Callout title="Why Recurring is its own type, not a setting on Full">
  A Full board asks "how far along is this job?" A Recurring board asks "when is the next visit, and
  did the last one happen?" The phase tracker, the completion state, and the archive rule all stop
  making sense on an open-ended contract, so the two share the board shell and little else.
</Callout>
