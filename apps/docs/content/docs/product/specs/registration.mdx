## Registration

### Overview

The registration flow creates a new user account and a workspace. The person registering becomes the workspace owner. No payment is required - all new workspaces start on the free tier.

---

### Flow

1. **Select industry** - choose from a predefined list that determines which templates are recommended during onboarding:
   - Automotive
   - Home Services
   - Personal Items & Repair (watches, jewellery, electronics, etc.)
   - Construction & Renovation
   - Creative & Design
   - Technology
   - Other
2. **Select provider type** - describes the workspace's setup:
   - Individual (solo operator, no registered business)
   - Business (registered entity, any size)
3. **Enter first and last name** - two separate required fields. The account stores both, and the full name is composed from them rather than entered on its own
4. **Enter business name** _(optional)_ - if left blank, the person name is used as the workspace display name
5. **Confirm the workspace address** - the display name from the previous step is slugified into a slug field that stays editable, and availability is checked as it changes. The slug identifies the workspace in URLs
6. **Enter contact info** - email (required) and phone number (optional). This is the user's personal contact info; workspace-level contact info can be configured later in settings.
7. **Create password** - the account password is chosen here, in the flow, and confirmed in a second field
8. **Marketing consent** _(optional)_ - an unticked checkbox for product news. Nothing else subscribes an account, and leaving it alone is a complete answer
9. **Review and submit**

---

### Rules

- Email is the primary user account identifier and must be unique
- Marketing email is **opt-in**. The checkbox starts unticked, an account that never ticks it joins no marketing segment, and a consent once given is only withdrawable by the person who gave it - no later change to their workspaces can revoke or re-assert it
- First and last name are both required, and neither may be blank. They are what the workspace member's display name is seeded from on a first join, and what the Resend contact carries, so a single free-text name field would not do
- Industry selection is used for template recommendations only - it does not lock the workspace into a category; any template can be used regardless of selected industry
- Provider type (Individual / Business) is stored for future use (invoicing, branding) but does not affect available features at this stage
- The slug is seeded from the workspace display name and remains editable. The availability check has to agree with what workspace creation accepts, so a reserved or protected slug reports as unavailable rather than free
- Slugification folds an accented character to its base letter before removing anything else, so `Açaí Manutenção` becomes `acai-manutencao` rather than dropping the accented letters. One transform in `@ordre/core` defines this, and both the registration flow and the API schema use it
- A display name that slugifies to nothing, such as punctuation alone or a non-Latin script, is not rejected as a name. The flow asks for a slug directly instead
- Leaving the business name blank means the person's name becomes the workspace display name, and so the public slug
- The password is set during registration, so the account is usable as soon as the form is submitted - a forgotten password is recovered through the reset link on the login page
- The workspace owner can later invite additional members (admin or member roles) from the dashboard
